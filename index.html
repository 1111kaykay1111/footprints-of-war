How the tour works

  1. Visit the stops in order. They follow the timeline.
  2. Read what happened, then study the evidence.
  3. Fly to the real place in Google Earth and watch the film or source.
  4. Answer the task questions. Your answers save on this device.
  5. Pass the quick check to earn a passport stamp.
  6. Finish with the chronology challenge and copy your answers to hand in.

By the end you can

  • explain the events that led to the rise of Nazi Germany
  • place key events of World War II in chronological order
  • interpret photographs, documents, graphs and maps as evidence
  • explain causes and consequences of key events
  • describe how Nazi policies took away people's rights
  • recognise the experiences of different groups during the war
